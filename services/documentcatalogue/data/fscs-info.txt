Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) Information Sheet

Important Information About Compensation Arrangements

1. What is the FSCS?
The Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) is the UK's statutory deposit
insurance scheme. It is funded by the financial services industry and is an
independent body set up under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA).

2. Am I covered?
Most depositors, including individuals, small businesses, and charities, are covered
by the FSCS. Deposits made by the following are NOT covered:
  - Credit institutions and investment firms
  - Financial institutions
  - Insurance and reinsurance undertakings
  - Government departments and central administrative authorities
  - County, city and local councils (above GBP 85,000)

3. What is the coverage limit?
The FSCS protects eligible deposits up to GBP 85,000 per depositor, per authorised
firm. If you hold deposits in different accounts with A-Bank plc, those balances are
aggregated for the purposes of calculating the FSCS limit.

Joint accounts are treated differently: each account holder is covered up to
GBP 85,000 (so a joint account held by two people is effectively covered up to
GBP 170,000).

4. Temporary High Balances
If you have a temporary high balance (e.g., from a property sale, inheritance, or
insurance payout), you may be entitled to protection above the GBP 85,000 limit for
up to 6 months from the date the amount was first deposited. The maximum temporary
high balance protection is GBP 1,000,000.

5. How to Claim
If A-Bank plc were to fail, the FSCS aims to make payments within 7 working days.
You do not need to make a claim — the FSCS will contact you directly using the
information held by A-Bank plc.

For more information, visit: https://www.fscs.org.uk
