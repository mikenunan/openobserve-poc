Savings Account Terms and Conditions

1. Account Type
This Savings Account ("the Account") is an interest-bearing deposit account provided
by A-Bank plc. The Account is designed for personal savings and is not intended for
day-to-day transactional use.

2. Interest Rate
  a) The current Annual Equivalent Rate (AER) is variable and published on our website
  b) Interest is calculated daily and credited to your Account monthly
  c) We reserve the right to change the interest rate with 14 days' prior notice
  d) Changes to the Bank of England base rate may be reflected in your rate

3. Deposits
  a) Minimum opening deposit: GBP 1.00
  b) Maximum balance: GBP 1,000,000
  c) Deposits may be made via bank transfer, standing order, or internal transfer
  d) Cash deposits are not accepted for this account type

4. Withdrawals
  a) Withdrawals are permitted without notice
  b) A maximum of 6 withdrawals per calendar month is permitted
  c) Withdrawals exceeding the monthly limit may incur a fee of GBP 5.00 per withdrawal
  d) Withdrawals must be made to a nominated UK bank account in your name

5. Tax
Interest earned on this Account may be subject to UK Income Tax. If you are a basic
rate taxpayer, you may benefit from the Personal Savings Allowance (PSA) of up to
GBP 1,000 per tax year.

6. FSCS Protection
Eligible deposits in this Account are protected by the Financial Services Compensation
Scheme (FSCS) up to GBP 85,000 per depositor. See the FSCS Info Sheet for details.
